Company profile

Your brand rides on every mission you order — the logo marks the pin on the quiXzoom map, and tapping it shows who ordered the mission and why. When a mission is completed and approved, a signed credential is issued and forwarded to your own system.

Your integrations

Bring your own language-model key, team webhook or feedback endpoint. Keys are stored to be used and never echoed back — and 'verified' is set only when the provider's real server answers a probe.

Outbound deliveries

Every delivery attempt — pushes, credentials, filed exceptions — with the receiver's actual answer. Each POST carries an idempotency id, an HMAC signature and a schema version; your system can verify any delivery against /v1/deliveries/verify. The log never stores the body. A failed row can be retried — rebuilt fresh from its source, never replayed from a saved copy, and always under a new id.

Your own zoomers

Link employees' quiXzoom accounts by reference, or create an invite code they redeem during quiXzoom onboarding. Landvex stores references and role labels — never names or emails; identity and consent live with quiXzoom.

Generate your own delivery

Pick a dataset, a media format and a webhook — preview the exact payload, then save it as a scheduled push. A failed delivery never looks like a delivered one.
